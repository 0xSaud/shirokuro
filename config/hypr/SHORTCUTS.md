# Hyprland — Keyboard & Mouse Reference

`SUPER` = Windows key.  Bound to your setup as of 2026-05-18.

|---|---|

| `SUPER + Q` | Terminal (kitty) |

## The Quickshell bar
- **Left:** launcher button · temp · CPU% · RAM · disk free
- **Center:** workspaces (click to switch)
- **Right:** media · system tray · battery · volume · mic · clock · layout · power
- **Every chip** can be switched off individually in `SUPER + I` → Bar items.
- Volume opens Sound; **mic click mutes instantly**, scroll adjusts gain.
- Clock opens the calendar. A red **REC** chip appears while recording; click to stop.

## Fonts and text
- Chosen in `SUPER + I` → Font. Six families, each previewing in its own typeface,
  plus a monospace choice and a text-size scale.
- Only families with **static** faces are offered. Qt renders nothing at all for
  variable-only families, so Rubik, Adwaita Sans, Cantarell and Vazirmatn are
  deliberately excluded — they make text vanish rather than fall back.
- Current: **Open Sans**, picked for legibility at the small label sizes.
- All text colour comes from the theme. Secondary text uses `onSurfaceVariant`,
  never `outline` (that is the border role and too dim to read).

## Terminal
- kitty follows the theme, including the **full 16-colour ANSI ramp**. Real hues
  are kept (red is still red) but nudged toward the scheme so it belongs to it.
- Regenerated by `apply-colors.sh` on every wallpaper or colour change, and kitty
  is signalled to reload in place.

## Recording
- Saved to `~/Pictures/../Videos/Recordings` as MP4, via `wf-recorder`.
- Region, focused screen, or stop : keys above, or the Capture row in `SUPER + N`.

## Night light
- Off / Mild 5000K / Warm 4000K / Amber 3000K in `SUPER + N`, via `hyprsunset`.
  The choice is remembered and reapplied at login.

## The panels
Every user-facing menu is Quickshell. **rofi is no longer used at all** : launcher,
power menu, colours, wallpaper, GIF, settings, clipboard, Wi-Fi, Control Center,
music, notifications and the lock screen.
The old rofi scripts are still on disk, unbound, purely as rollback.

## Colours
- **Colours are generated from the wallpaper.** Change the wallpaper and the whole
  shell, window borders and kitty recolour to match. That is the point : you change
  wallpapers often, so nothing has to be matched by hand.
- A full **Material 3** palette is derived, not one accent: primary, secondary and
  tertiary plus container pairs and five surface levels. That is why the panels no
  longer read as a single flat colour.
- `SUPER + T` switches between "from the wallpaper" and a fixed colour you pick.
  Your 16 saved themes are still there, now used as *starting colours* that the
  generator builds a full palette around.
- Intensity (muted / natural / vivid / intense) and **harmony** are on the same screen.
- **Harmony** sets how far the third accent rotates from your colour. Material's own
  default is 60°, which turns an orange scheme yellow-green : that was the stray
  green. Default here is 30°, and 0° keeps everything in one family.
- Engine: `~/.config/themes/bin/gen-colors.py` (Lab/LCh with gamut mapping) driven
  by `apply-colors.sh`, which `apply-wallpaper.sh` calls automatically. Output lands
  in `~/.config/quickshell/colors.json` and every panel watches that file.

## Lock screen
- The Quickshell lock screen is now the **default**, authenticating through PAM
  (`/etc/pam.d/hyprlock`). Switch back to hyprlock any time in `SUPER + I`.
- Your chosen GIF appears as a **circular avatar** above the greeting, using the
  same framing you set in `SUPER + G`. With no GIF it falls back to your initial.
- Password unlock is verified working.
- **If it ever refuses your password:** from that TTY run
  `qs ipc call lock release`. That is the deliberate escape hatch.

## GIFs
- `SUPER + G` picks one. It shows in the Control Center header (behind the
  greeting, scrimmed so text stays readable) and as the music artwork when a track
  has no cover.
- GIFs live in `~/Pictures/gifs`. **Fit all** pulls the whole GIF into frame; scrolling
  down goes below 1.00x so tall or wide ones are not forced to fill.
  Each GIF keeps its own **framing** (zoom + pan),
  so a wide or tall one is not blindly centre-cropped. Saved in
  `~/.config/quickshell/gif.json` and applied live.

## Wallpapers
- `SUPER + W` : grid of `~/Pictures/Wallpapers`, plus "Add a wallpaper".
- Picking one sets it **and** regenerates the colour scheme from it.
- Choice is saved to `~/.config/themes/state.json` and restored on login.

## Rollback (if ever needed)
- Bar: re-enable `exec-once = waybar` and comment `exec-once = qs` in `~/.config/hypr/hyprland.conf` (Waybar config untouched).
- Hyprland config backup: `~/.config/hypr/hyprland.conf.bak`
